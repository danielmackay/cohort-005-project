# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to students. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
A person who buys and takes courses.
_Avoid_: Learner, Customer, User (for the person taking a course)

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member who runs the platform, including deciding Refund Requests. A course's own instructor is not an Admin for this purpose.
_Avoid_: The team, Staff (for the person deciding Refund Requests)

### Courses

**Enrollment**:
A student's one lasting link to a course, kept even after a Refund. Whether it gives access to the course is shown by whether it is active or refunded.

**Active Enrollment**:
An Enrollment that gives the student access to the course.

**Refunded Enrollment**:
An Enrollment whose purchase was refunded, so it gives no access. It becomes active again if the student buys the course again.
_Avoid_: Revoked, Cancelled, Unenrolled, Withdrawn

**Seat Coupon**:
A claimable seat on a course, bought by a student for someone on their team.
_Avoid_: Voucher, Code, Licence

**Voided Seat Coupon**:
A Seat Coupon that can no longer be claimed because the purchase it came from was refunded.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A student's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it, and that decision is final. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Admin has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and it ends the student's access to the course straight away. The student's progress is kept, out of sight, and returns if they buy the course again.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down, with a reason the student can see. It has no Refund, and the student may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a student after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund. A student gets at most one Refund per course, so buying a course again after a Refund can't be refunded.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the student.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
