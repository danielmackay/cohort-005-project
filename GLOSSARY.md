# Course Platform

An online course platform where learners buy courses, watch lessons, and can ask for their money back shortly after buying.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with a reason in their own words, to get their money back for a Purchase they made themselves; staff review every one by hand.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request that staff have not yet Approved or Declined. A Purchase has at most one at a time.
_Avoid_: Pending Chargeback, pending reversal

**Approved**:
The outcome of a Refund Request where staff agree to send money back, producing a Refund.

**Declined**:
The outcome of a Refund Request where staff refuse it; no money goes back.

**Refund**:
Money actually sent back to the learner for a Purchase. It exists only once a Refund Request is Approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Amount**:
The amount the learner paid for the Purchase, whatever discount applied at the time.
_Avoid_: list price refund

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase may have a Refund Request raised against it. It requires all of: inside the Refund Window, under half the course's lessons completed, under two hours watched, not a claimed team seat, and no earlier Declined Refund Request.

**Refunded Purchase**:
A Purchase whose Refund Request was Approved. It no longer gives the learner access to the course.
_Avoid_: cancelled purchase, reversed purchase

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days from the day of the Purchase during which a Refund Request can be raised.
_Avoid_: cancel window
